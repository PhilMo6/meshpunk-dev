The games in this folder are the example apps that ship with Pyxel 2.9.9
(https://github.com/kitao/pyxel, python/pyxel/examples/apps), unmodified.
Each one is released by its author under the MIT License below.

30sec_of_daylight.pyxapp  30 Seconds of Daylight, by Adam
                          https://github.com/kitao/30SecondsOfDaylight
cursed_caverns.pyxapp     Cursed Caverns, by Takashi Kitao
laser-jetman.pyxapp       Laser Jetman, Copyright (c) 2022 helpcomputer (Adam)
                          https://github.com/helpcomputer/laser-jetman
mega_wing.pyxapp          Mega Wing, by Takashi Kitao
megaball.pyxapp           Megaball, by Adam
                          https://github.com/helpcomputer/megaball
space_rescue.pyxapp       Space Rescue, by Takashi Kitao
vortexion.pyxapp          Vortexion, Copyright (c) 2022 helpcomputer (Adam)
                          https://github.com/helpcomputer/vortexion
